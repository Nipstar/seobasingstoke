AI SEO and GEO

The map shows where you rank. AI search is a separate question

Someone asking ChatGPT or Google's AI for a Basingstoke firm never sees a grid, so this is scoped as its own piece of work. AI SEO deals with Google and AI Overviews; GEO, or generative engine optimisation, deals with tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini. Both are priced to scope after a call. Nobody can guarantee a citation in an AI answer, and nothing can be promised about Google's AI answers.

The monthly report

One grid, your position at every point

A map-rank grid answers one question: if someone searched from this exact spot, where would you appear? LocalFalcon lays a grid of points over the area you cover and records your Google Maps position at each one, which shows where a Basingstoke listing holds up and where it falls away.

Map results change with the searcher's location. Ranking well from your own premises says very little about how you look from Chineham or Popley, and a single average number hides that.

The summary that comes with the grid says in plain words what was done that month. If a position did not move, it says so.

Questions about rankings and reports

How long does local SEO take in Basingstoke?

Months, not weeks. How quickly it moves depends on your reviews and profile today, so that is what Andy looks at first.

What is a map-rank grid?

A set of points spread across the area you cover. At each point LocalFalcon records where your business appears in Google Maps for a chosen search, so you see your position street by street rather than as one average.

Do I need a new website as well?

Not always, because your Google profile and reviews do much of the work on the map. If the site itself is holding you back, Basingstoke website design is the same business, with builds from £600.

Is there a contract?

Terms are agreed in writing before work starts.

What does GEO mean?

Generative engine optimisation. It is the work of making your business easy for tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini to read and quote: clear answers on key pages, structured data and consistent business facts. It is priced to scope, and citations cannot be guaranteed.

How does AI SEO relate to my map rankings?

They overlap but they are measured differently. The grid records your Google Maps position from each point, while AI SEO looks at how Google and AI Overviews read your site and what they can quote from it. Improving one does not guarantee a change in the other.

One person. Antek Automation is a sole trader, so there is no account team between you and Andy, and he is based in Andover rather than in the town.
